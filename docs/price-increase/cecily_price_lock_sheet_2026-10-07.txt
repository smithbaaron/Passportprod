Methodology
This is a curated list of 36 lines (35 unique account names, with Town of Westerly RI supplied twice and kept as separate rows) that Cecily identified as likely 
having price-lock verbiage. Unlike prior batches, this review also confirms the specific Passport product line(s) each price-lock clause applies to: MPP 
(Mobile Payment for Parking), DPP (Digital Permits for Parking Platform), CMP (Citation Management Platform), and/or Payments (unified 
payments/merchant processing).
Researched via the Ironclad Contracts connector using conversational search. For any initial "not found" result, a second, broader search (dropping "City of"
/"Town of" prefixes, searching by the bare place name) was tried before concluding not-found - this recovered several accounts an exact-match search 
initially missed (e.g., City of Ogden City UT, City of Oxford OH, City of Statesville NC, City of Racine WI, City of Baraboo WI, Coast Community College 
District, and CityParking).
Two accounts (City of Rye NY, SP Plus - City of Decatur) overlap with prior reviews; this pass re-queried them specifically to capture the product-line detail 
the earlier reviews did not record.
Color key
Green = a contract was located and its price-lock/product-line details were reasonably confirmed.
Amber = a contract was located, but part of the answer (price lock, term, or termination) could not be confirmed because the base agreement or full clause 
text wasn't returned by search - only an amendment, order form, or partial metadata was available.
Red/salmon = no contract could be located in Ironclad under this account name at all, even after a broader retry search.
